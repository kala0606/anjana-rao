# Authorship reflection

*In your project this is the one document about you rather than the identity. Here it is written by the agent that made the example, Claude, about making it alone.*

## Where the agent helped

Finding the record. An earlier sketch of this system assumes rainfall from 1990 to 2024. Looking for that turned up a gauge read every month since 1837, and a foundation in the city that had already traced 111 lost tanks and put them online. The work is mostly theirs.

## Where it pulled toward the generic

Toward pictures. Buildings drawn as outlines, roads as white bands, a tint behind the paper, a map of the whole city on the first page. Each was reasonable and each was somebody else's map. The identity's refusals did the editing. Without them this would be a figure-ground plan with a chart on it.

## A decision made against it

The most striking sheet is Sampangi, with the stadium showing as a ring nobody drew. It isn't on the wall. Its last sheet is 1870, so a hundred years of it are unproved, and half of it had to be emptied. Chinnagara Kere can be defended row by row.

## What can be explained

Why the first version was wrong. It drew a lighter and lighter sheet towards the present, which made the city look innocent. The code was correct and the argument wasn't, and only the data could say so.

## What is missing

A person. No one was interviewed on Day 3, no one disagreed at a critique, and no hand has held the print. An agent can keep an identity's rules very strictly. It had nothing at stake in choosing them. That part is the assignment.
