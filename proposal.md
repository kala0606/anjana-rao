# Proposal · Anjana Rao

*One page, Day 5. Kept as it was submitted; Week 2 proved part of it wrong.*

## Who is speaking

Anjana Rao is a land surveyor in 2041, still sent to measure Bengaluru's irrigation tanks, which exist only in the records. She holds that the city maps what it owns and never what it has lost, and she draws the second map.

## The condition

The loss of the city's tanks to layouts, stadiums and roads since the 1970s, and the groundwater decline that followed. Evidence: 111 lost tanks traced from old sheets (ref. 1); 187 years of one rain gauge (ref. 2); the state auditor's finding that demarcation of the lakes remained incomplete (ref. 8).

## An initial generative concept

The survey grid as a sieve. The system takes in a lost tank's boundary and the rain record. It lays one row of chain lines across the bed for each year, and draws the row heavier the further that year's rain fell short. It puts out one survey sheet per tank.

## A body for it

Archival print on matte cotton rag, A2, black only, with a pen plot of the same sheet as the alternate. Cotton because the old sheets that survived are on cotton; matte because a record shouldn't shine.

## What I still need to find out

Whether the rain did fall short. And what is standing on each bed now, recorded by someone other than me.
