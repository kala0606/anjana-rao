# References

Every link was opened, and every dataset downloaded, on 22 November 2026.

## The condition

1. **Lost Tanks** and **Existing Tanks** — Mod Foundation, Bengaluru Watershed download centre. https://mod-foundation.github.io/download_center/
   Why: the boundaries. 111 tanks traced from sheets of 1854, 1870, 1897 and 1969. Its own disclaimer says it is not an on-the-ground survey; she has to carry that warning too.
2. **GHCN-Monthly v4 precipitation**, station IN009010100 Bangalore (WMO 43295) — NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/ghcnm/v4/precipitation/doc/ghcn-m_v4_prcp_readme.txt
   Why: 187 complete years of one gauge. It is the only witness that was there the whole time.
3. **The Lost Lakes of Bangalore** — Hita Unnikrishnan and Harini Nagendra, *Arcadia* 13, 2018. https://www.environmentandsociety.org/arcadia/lost-lakes-bangalore
   Why: how a tank city became a city with lakes, then without them.
4. **The Lake That Became a Sports Stadium** — Unnikrishnan and Nagendra, *Arcadia* 23, 2018. https://www.environmentandsociety.org/arcadia/lake-became-sports-stadium
   Why: Sampangi, one tank followed from 1885 to the rectangle that is left.
5. **Imperilled Waterscapes** — Amrita Sen, Hita Unnikrishnan and Harini Nagendra, *Ecology, Economy and Society*, 2020. https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/163963/
   Why: tanks as a connected system, so losing one is never losing one.
6. **Central and state surveys throw up same, grim result** — ThePrint, 6 June 2025, reporting the Groundwater Directorate. https://theprint.in/environment/central-state-surveys-throw-up-same-grim-result-bengaluru-groundwater-depleting-at-dangerous-pace/2643932
   Why: two measured depths to water in the east, nine years apart.
7. **Bengaluru's groundwater conundrum** — Deccan Herald, 13 January 2024. https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/bengaluru-s-groundwater-conundrum-2847211
   Why: the thousand-foot figure, spoken by residents and not measured.
8. **Performance audit on conservation of lakes**, executive summary — Comptroller and Auditor General, Karnataka Report 1 of 2015. https://cag.gov.in/uploads/download_audit_report/2015/Karnataka_Report_1_2015_exc_sum.pdf
   Why: the state's own auditor recording that survey and demarcation of the lakes it checked "remained incomplete".

## Lineage

9. **Dividing Line** (2001) — Zarina, woodcut. Whitney Museum. https://whitney.org/collection/works/46388
   Why: a border as one heavy black line cut by hand. What she refuses to draw.
10. **Deccan Traverses: The Making of Bangalore's Terrain** — Anuradha Mathur and Dilip da Cunha, 2006. https://www.mathurdacunha.com/deccan-traverses
    Why: surveying as something done to Bangalore, not just a picture of it.
11. **Shapolsky et al. Manhattan Real Estate Holdings** (1971) — Hans Haacke. Whitney Museum. https://whitney.org/collection/works/29487
    Why: public records as the whole argument. No commentary added.
12. **Great Trigonometrical Survey.** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Trigonometrical_Survey
    Why: her profession's founding act, and what it was for.
13. **Hanne Darboven.** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanne_Darboven
    Why: writing time out by hand, number by number.

## Materials and methods

14. **Gunter's chain.** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunter%27s_chain
    Why: what a chain is, how long, how carried. Where the station spacing comes from.
15. **Point in polygon.** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_in_polygon
    Why: the one test the whole system rests on: is this station inside the bed, or inside a wall?
16. **Hershey fonts** (A. V. Hershey, 1967). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hershey_fonts
    Why: letters made of single pen strokes, so the plotter can write every row's date.
17. **OpenStreetMap: copyright and licence.** https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
    Why: the walls and roads, and how to credit the people who drew them.

## Manifestos

18. **Sentences on Conceptual Art** — Sol LeWitt, 1969. https://www.altx.com/vizarts/conceptual.html
    Why: numbered sentences that behave like rules.
19. **Manifesto for Maintenance Art, 1969!** — Mierle Laderman Ukeles. https://feldmangallery.com/exhibition/manifesto-for-maintenance-art-1969
    Why: upkeep as work, and the question of who is meant to do it. A tank is kept, or it isn't.
