# Authorship log

What the agent suggested, what was kept, what was rejected and why. In this worked example the agent, Claude, both proposed and decided, against the identity's rules; the entries are choices that came up in the making, set against the day they would fall on. In your project the deciding voice is yours, and that difference is the point of this file.

## Day 03

**Suggested:** five metaphors, four of them with water in them.
**Kept:** the sieve.
**Rejected, and why:** rain as agents running downhill. It would draw water nobody measured.
**What this says about the identity:** she isn't mourning a lake. She is filing a return.

## Day 04

**Suggested:** gridded or reanalysis rainfall, because it has no gaps and covers every tank.
**Kept:** one gauge.
**Rejected, and why:** a modelled value is not a reading. One instrument in one place, read for 187 years, is weaker as science about a particular tank and stronger as testimony, and the sheet says which it is.
**What this says about the identity:** she would rather be narrow than invented.

## Day 06

**Suggested:** draw the buildings lightly, as context.
**Kept:** nothing.
**Rejected, and why:** every other map already draws them. The only thing she knows about a building is that her chain couldn't go through it.
**What this says about the identity:** presence is what the city draws. She draws what it stopped.

## Day 08

**Suggested:** the first plan, taken from an earlier sketch of this system: rows from 1990 to 2024, heavier as the rain falls short.
**Kept:** the rows, and the rule that absence is heavier.
**Rejected, and why:** the gauge rose over those years, so the plan drew the city as blameless. Choosing years to fit the argument would have hidden it. The argument had to change instead: the rain did not fail, it was not kept.
**What this says about the identity:** a rule that can't prove its owner wrong isn't a rule.

## Day 09 · the hero

**Suggested:** Sampangi (seed 4). Everyone in the city knows the stadium, and it makes the most striking sheet.
**Kept:** Chinnagara Kere (seed 1).
**Rejected, and why:** Sampangi's last sheet in this register is 1870, so for a century nothing proves what was there. Chinnagara is on the sheet of 1969. She hangs the sheet that can be defended, not the one that will be photographed.
**What this says about the identity:** evidence before recognition.

## Day 09 · the manifesto

**Suggested:** keep "past a thousand feet"; it is the strongest line and a newspaper supports it.
**Kept:** the two depths the Groundwater Directorate measured.
**Rejected, and why:** the newspaper reports people saying it. Rule one was already written.
**What this says about the identity:** her rules apply to her first.
