An Atlas of Elsewhere. Identity

Anjana Rao

Surveyor of tank beds

Origin
Born 1999, in a village whose tank was built over in 2004. Her father kept its boundary stones.
Stands
Role and time. A government land surveyor in 2041, sent to measure tanks that exist only in the records.
Condition
The loss of Bengaluru's irrigation tanks since the 1970s, and the falling water table that followed.
Works in
Chain survey, the public record, black ink on cotton paper.

I measure what this city has agreed to forget.
