# Deficit Survey, Tank No. 74

Anjana Rao is a surveyor of tank beds in 2041, measuring irrigation tanks that Bengaluru has built over and stopped drawing.

This A2 print surveys Chinnagara Kere, now Ejipura. Each row is a year of rain, from 1901 to 2023, with its date and source in the margin. The bed was last drawn in 1969.

A system ruled every line. While the tank was on the sheet, a row darkens when the rain fell short. After 1969 it darkens with all the rain that fell, because none was kept, and it stops at every building standing there now. Nobody drew the buildings. They are where the measuring stopped.

The rain did not fail. The work asks who was meant to keep it, and whether you are standing on a tank now.

Worked example, 2026. Made for Computationally Yours: Out of One's Place.
