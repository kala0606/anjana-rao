# Three candidate identities

Day 2. One paragraph each, each tied to a real condition.

## 1 · Anjana Rao, surveyor of tank beds

Condition: the loss of Bengaluru's irrigation tanks since the 1970s, and the falling water table after it. A government surveyor in 2041 still sent to measure tanks that exist only in the records. From there the map itself becomes the evidence: what it stopped drawing, and when. Evidence to hand: old sheets, a rain gauge, encroachment audits.

## 2 · Devika Nair, keeper of the night roster

Condition: Bengaluru's night-shift work, whose hours are set by another country's clock. She draws up the rosters for a support floor, and is the only person who sees everyone's missing sleep on one page. From there the city has two populations that never meet. Evidence to find: shift rosters, transport records, occupational health studies.

## 3 · Lokesh Gowda, clerk of the borewell register

Condition: private borewells drilled in the eastern taluks, few of them recorded. He keeps a register that nobody is made to fill in. From there depletion looks like an empty column. Evidence to find: registration numbers, water-table readings, drilling receipts.

## The choice

Anjana Rao. The third has the same condition, but no data would mean no system. The second is a real position, but I would be speaking for people I hadn't asked. Anjana's evidence is public, old, and already disagrees with the city's map.
