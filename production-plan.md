# Production plan · Anjana Rao

*One page, Day 10.*

**Pathway.** Archival print, A2, one sheet. Alternate: the same seed as an A2 pen plot, if a slot is free.

**The sheet.** Seed 1, all dials at default. Deficit Survey, Tank No. 74, Chinnagara Kere, 1:2000.

**Why it needs this.** Tone isn't needed; density and small letters are. 123 rows of 1.5 mm writing beside solid black bars is hard on a pen and easy on a printer. The print is the record, the plot is the act.

**If it goes wrong.** Writing fills in on the rag: raise it from 1.5 to 1.8 mm in rule 7 and print again, never by hand. No plotter slot: the print stands alone.
