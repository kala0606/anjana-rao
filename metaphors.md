# Metaphor log

Day 3. Five computational metaphors proposed by the coding agent from Parts A, B and E of the identity worksheet. No code.

1. **The survey grid as a sieve.** Each cell keeps the water that should be there; the drawing records the deficit. *Could fail:* if every cell is treated alike it becomes a heat map.
2. **Sediment.** Each year lays a stratum; lost years leave a gap. *Could fail:* strata are restful. Geology forgives.
3. **Agents as rain.** Drops walk downhill and collect where a tank was. *Could fail:* it invents water, which rule 1 forbids.
4. **Erasure.** Start with a full sheet and remove what has been built over. *Could fail:* it makes absence lighter than presence, which is rule 2 backwards.
5. **Cellular automaton of encroachment.** Buildings spread into the bed from its edge. *Could fail:* it simulates a history that can be looked up.

**Kept: 1, the sieve.** She would choose it because it is a thing she already does. A chain line is a sieve: it goes across the ground and keeps only what she could actually reach.

**What the agent got wrong.** It read her as mourning. Four of the five wanted water to appear, or the bed to look lovely as it went. She is not mourning, she is filing a return.
